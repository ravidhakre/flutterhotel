THINGS TO DO IN LANSDOWNE
SEO Meta Title
Things To Do in Lansdowne | Flutter Hotels  (42 chars)
Meta Description
Temples, viewpoints, a war memorial and boating near Flutter Hotels &amp; Resorts, Lansdowne. Plan your sightseeing from our hillside stay.  (135 chars)

Page Headline (H1):
Things To Do In Lansdowne
Hero Subheadline:
A quiet hill station with a handful of unmissable stops — all within easy reach of the resort.
Lansdowne rewards slow travel. Beyond the resort, you’ll find temples, viewpoints, a colonial-era church, a war memorial, a waterfall and a lake — each an easy trip from Flutter Hotels &amp; Resorts. Here’s what to add to your itinerary.
Tarkeshwar Mahadev Temple
A revered Shiva temple set among the hills — a peaceful stop for morning darshan or a quiet walk.
Tip-in-Top Point (Tiffin Top)
Lansdowne’s well-known viewpoint for sunrise and sunset, with sweeping views over the surrounding Garhwal hills.
St. Mary’s Church
Built in 1895, this colonial-era church is one of Lansdowne’s oldest landmarks — worth a visit for its history and architecture.
Garhwal Rifles Regimental War Memorial
A memorial honouring the Garhwal Rifles regiment — a meaningful stop for anyone interested in military history.
Bhalugarh Waterfalls
A scenic waterfall on the outskirts of town, a good half-day trip for those who enjoy a short trek.
Bhulla Tal Lake
A small lake in the heart of Lansdowne offering boating — a relaxed way to spend an afternoon with family.
Getting Here
Nearest airport: Dehradun (Jolly Grant Airport)
Nearest railway station: Kotdwara
